Undergraduate dissertation

Dietary Diversity and Household Food Insecurity as Risk Factors Associated with Musculoskeletal Disorders among Ready-Made Garment Workers in Bangladesh: A Cross-Sectional Study

This community-based cross-sectional study was conducted in Sreepur Upazila, Gazipur, Bangladesh. The final analytic sample comprised 402 participants.

The study assessed dietary diversity using the Individual Dietary Diversity Score (IDDS), household food insecurity using the Food Insecurity Experience Scale (FIES), and factors associated with musculoskeletal disorders.

Cross-sectional design 402 participants Sreepur, Gazipur
