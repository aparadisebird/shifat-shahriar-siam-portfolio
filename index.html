Research & writing

Evidence before assumptions.

My undergraduate dissertation examined the relationships between dietary diversity, household food insecurity, and musculoskeletal disorders among garment workers in Bangladesh.

UNDERGRADUATE DISSERTATION

Dietary Diversity and Household Food Insecurity as Risk Factors Associated with Musculoskeletal Disorders among Ready-Made Garment Workers in Bangladesh: A Cross-Sectional Study

Study setting: Sreepur Upazila, Gazipur, Bangladesh. Analytic sample: 402 participants.

The research used a cross-sectional design and examined dietary diversity, household food insecurity, and factors associated with musculoskeletal disorders.

Full abstract, methods, results, and research materials will be added after the content has been reviewed for publication.
